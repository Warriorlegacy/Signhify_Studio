## Review Collection Strategy

Reviews are the currency of directory listings. Without them, Clutch and
GoodFirms won't rank you.

**Process**:
1. After every client project completion → send review request email
2. Ask for review on a specific platform (rotate: Clutch, GoodFirms, Trustpilot)
3. Make it easy: provide a direct link and a suggested review outline
4. Offer an incentive: 10% off next engagement for a published review

**Suggested Review Request**:

> Hi [Client],
>
> Thanks again for choosing Signhify for your [project].
>
> If you're happy with the result, would you mind leaving a quick review on
> [Clutch / GoodFirms / Trustpilot]? It helps us tremendously as a young studio.
>
> Here's the direct link: [URL]
>
> Even 3-4 sentences about your experience would be amazing.
>
> As a thank-you, I'll give you 10% off your next Sprint or Studio engagement.
>
> Best,
> Piyush
