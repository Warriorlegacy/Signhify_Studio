# Social Post — AI Social Media Manager

Three-phase workflow. Learns your voice from actual posts, builds a content calendar, generates and publishes platform-native posts.

## Phase Workflow

| Phase | What it does |
|-------|-------------|
| **P1 — Learn Style** | Scans your existing Facebook posts, analyzes voice/tone patterns, builds a style profile |
| **P0 — Plan Calendar** | Creates 14-day content calendar with post types, platforms, and timing |
| **P2 — Generate & Publish** | Writes native posts per platform, formatted and ready |

## Guidelines

- One post per day max (Mode B)
- Best posting window: 22:00-01:00 (AI audience is nocturnal)
- Content mix: 40% value, 20% personal, 15% engagement, 15% promotion, 10% curated
- FB/Threads: never include external links in body text
- Keyword-based CTAs (e.g. "comment 'X'") perform best
- No AI-sounding abstractions — be real, be specific
- Demo > claim: concrete results beat abstract "I'm good"

## Usage

```
Take this idea and give me a week of posts for Instagram, Threads and X in my voice, plus the best time to post each one.
[paste your idea]
```
