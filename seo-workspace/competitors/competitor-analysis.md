## 🎯 Top 3 Lessons & Counter-Positioning

1. **Own the "Vibe Coding" & "AI Swarm" Vocabulary**:
   Competitors are still optimizing for "web design agency". Signhify captures modern AI-native searchers who search specifically for AI engineering, vibe coding, and prompt-to-product studios.
2. **Leverage Code-First Assets for Domain Authority**:
   By keeping NPM packages and GitHub repositories active with links pointing to `https://signhify.dpdns.org`, Signhify earns organic developer backlinks that traditional agencies cannot replicate.
3. **Turn Case Studies into Programmatic Landing Pages**:
   Competitors show static PDF portfolios. Signhify has dynamic project pages ([`/projects/$slug`](file:///d:/Signhify/src/routes/projects.%24slug.tsx)) indexed in `sitemap.xml` for each niche (CRM, AI Reels, Fitness SaaS, Micro-SaaS).
